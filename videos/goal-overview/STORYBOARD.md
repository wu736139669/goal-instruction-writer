## Video direction

Use code-editorial's warm cream, ink, terracotta and local serif/sans/mono
voices. English primary titles, readable Simplified Chinese secondary captions.
Flat editorial composition with purposeful line draws and reveals, then held
reads. Four clean cuts, no element continues across a seam. The gameplay is
real, ungraded footage; source controls the pixels. All other motion is a
labeled explanation of the skill, not fabricated chat or terminal history.
The 60+ hour report is attributed on screen. Do not imply this game excerpt is
the source of that report. Silence is intentional; no SCRIPT.md or audio cues.
