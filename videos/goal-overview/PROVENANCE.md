The frame-1 counter/track was adapted from the HyperFrames `mk-progress-stat`
primitive and its documented counting recipe to carry an attributed field
report rather than an invented progress metric. The phase rail is conceptual,
not a time axis.

The game frame was inspected before assembly. Its colors and viewport are kept
as recorded; no cosmetic treatment is needed for the material to be legible.
The clip is played at its original speed and is muted because it has no audio
track. No private source repository, raw SDK bundle, credentials, or machine
paths are included as video content.

The 36-second MP4 is the full demonstration. The README GIF is a shorter excerpt
of that render. It remains an excerpt, not a time-lapse of a long agent run.
